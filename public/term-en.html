English Version — Consumer Notice

1. Returns and Refunds

Basic Rules

  • CASHOP supports returns and refunds under limited periods and conditions.
  • In the case of major defects (e.g., obvious damage, incorrect model/specification/quantity, visible stains, etc.), CASHOP will assist in returning the item to the merchant or handling the issue.
  • For user-related reasons (e.g., wrong purchase, change of mind, etc.), the return/refund request must be submitted during the “Package Inspection” stage. The return conditions are subject to the merchant’s policy, which CASHOP will communicate and assist with.

Application Period

  • From the day after “Package Inspection” is completed and the product status changes to “Listed”, users may apply for the return/refund within five (5) days including weekends.
  • Once the user submits the “Shipment Request”, returns and refunds will no longer be accepted.
  • The platform’s system record time shall prevail; unless otherwise stated, the time zone is UTC/GMT+08:00. The 5-day period starts at 00:00 on the day following “Listing” and ends at 23:59:59 on the fifth day.

Return Costs

  • Except for major defects caused by CASHOP or third-party merchants, all domestic shipping and necessary return costs shall be borne by the user. For returns/refunds caused by CASHOP, relevant costs shall be borne by CASHOP in accordance with applicable laws.

2. International Logistics Risks and Related Costs

Risk Transfer

  • Once the goods are dispatched and handed over to the carrier, the risk of loss or damage transfers to the user. The carrier’s compensation responsibility is governed by its own transport terms.

Cost Description

  • Customs duties, import consumption tax (VAT), customs clearance service fees, and insurance shall be borne by the user as the Importer of Record. Purchasing shipping insurance is recommended.
  • If the platform collects and pays related fees on behalf of the user, such fees will be clearly shown on the settlement page and follow the carrier/customs standards.

3. Customs Clearance Cooperation Obligations

As the Importer of Record, the recipient must truthfully declare and actively cooperate with customs clearance (including payment of taxes and submission of required documents). If the user refuses or delays cooperation, resulting in failed delivery, return, or destruction, all related warehouse fees, re-delivery fees, return fees, or disposal fees shall be borne by the user. The carrier’s liability shall follow its transport terms.

4. Standard Differences Notice

Products sold on the platform generally comply with the quality, safety, hygiene, environmental, and labeling standards of their country of origin. However, they may differ from those of the destination country/region.
Before placing an order, users must confirm that the product is legally eligible for personal import and not among prohibited or restricted categories in Japan.
Any detention, return, or destruction due to violation of destination laws will be borne by the Importer of Record. CASHOP reserves the right to refuse handling of items that are explicitly prohibited or restricted by law.
